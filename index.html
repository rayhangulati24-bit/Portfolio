Projects

1Self-driven build - energy-harvesting shoe

StepCharge

A shoe that harvests energy from walking. Syringe pumps in the sole compress with each step and push water through sub-pipes into a main pipe; the pressure spins a fin attached to a small motor, generating enough electricity to light an LED every step. Designed on paper, then prototyped: an early version used a copper coil on the toe.
